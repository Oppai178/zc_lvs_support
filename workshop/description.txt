[h1][Z-CITY] LVS SUPPORT[/h1]
[b]Author: Oppai[/b]

Compatibility layer so [b]LVS[/b] works with Z-City systems: fake/ragdoll, organism, camera, TPIK, weapons in seats.

Glide is not touched. Z-City files are not patched. Works on the whole LVS framework, not on a single pack.

[h2]Required[/h2]
[list]
[*] Z-City
[*] [LVS] - Framework
[*] any LVS car / helicopter pack
[/list]

Tested first with [b][LVS] Vehicles from Snowdrop escape Swelter[/b] and [b][LVS] - Helicopters[/b].

[h2]Cars (Snowdrop, LVS Cars, any lvs_base_wheeldrive)[/h2]
[list]
[*] Enter → hg.Fake, ragdoll welded to the LVS chassis
[*] Z-City camera
[*] Hands on the wheel if the model has a steering bone
[*] Weapons in the cabin. R reloads; engine toggle only with empty hands
[*] Two-handed guns slow steering and throttle
[*] Passing out does not eject you; driver inputs are idled
[*] Hard crash can throw you out; crash/explosion hits the organism
[*] Loose ragdolls and small props stay in the cargo until an LVS door is open
[*] Climb into the rear cargo of a van without a seat (open the rear door, then E)
[*] Bullets go through glass / open doorways into the cabin
[*] Gunner/turret seats use LVS aim camera; the driver keeps Z-City camera
[*] Burning / wrecked engine: heat soaks the organism; a dead engine will not start
[*] Loose bodies stay lying in the cargo (van etc.) while doors are closed
[/list]

[h2]Tanks / closed vehicles[/h2]
[list]
[*] LVS hatch / tank camera
[*] Fake ragdoll stays at the hatch/seat (hull no longer shoves the body outside)
[*] Works with zc_lvs_fake 0 — tank aim is not zeroed
[/list]

[h2]Helicopters[/h2]
[list]
[*] Fake ragdoll in the seat
[*] LVS camera and mouse-aim from eye height
[*] Alt-look: head follows the camera, LVS reticle stays on the last aim
[*] Seat switch at speed no longer yeets you while the camera stays in the cabin
[*] Organism still applies
[/list]

[h2]Planes / fighters[/h2]
[list]
[*] Own LVS flight camera (not the helicopter look-cam)
[*] Landing-gear rumble on takeoff is not a crash — you stay in the seat
[*] Mouse-aim flies the plane; direct-input is not overwritten
[/list]

[h2]Controls[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]1–0[/b] — vehicle weapons
[*] [b]Alt + 1–0[/b] — change seat (Alt+1 as driver = lock)
[*] [b]E[/b] — exit
[*] [b]R[/b] — reload if you hold a gun; engine on/off only with hands
[*] Shift / Alt while seated no longer trigger Z-City climbing / eject
[/list]
